The Story

One developer.
Four worlds.
Zero shortcuts.

Most online arcades are templates wearing different skins. Yuvraj Soni Arcade is different — every game here is written, tuned, and shipped by a single person, end to end, with no asset packs and no recycled engines.

The goal isn't the most games. It's to have four that feel considered — distinct mechanics, distinct moods, and a shared profile system that ties your progress into one evolving identity.

The Loop

How the arcade works

One profile, one XP bar — every game feeds into it.

01

Pick a game

Choose from four titles, each with its own pace and skill curve. No installs — open and play instantly in any browser.

02

Earn XP & coins

Every run feeds your single arcade-wide profile. Performance in any game contributes to the same progress bar.

03

Level up forever

Your level climbs as XP builds. Saved locally — exactly as you left it, every time you return.
