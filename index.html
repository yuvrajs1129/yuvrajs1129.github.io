Why This Exists

One developer.
Four worlds.
Zero shortcuts.

Most arcades online are templates wearing different skins. Yuvraj Soni Arcade is the opposite — every game here is written, tuned, and shipped by a single person, end to end, with no asset packs and no recycled engines.

The goal isn't to have the most games. It's to have four that actually feel considered — distinct mechanics, distinct moods, and a profile system that ties your progress across all of them.

The Loop

How the Arcade Works

One profile, one XP bar, every game feeds into it.

01

Pick a game

Choose from four titles, each with its own pace and skill curve. No installs, no loading screens — just open and play.

02

Earn XP & coins

Every run you finish feeds your single arcade-wide profile. Performance in any game contributes to the same progress bar.

03

Level up

Your level climbs automatically as XP builds. It's saved locally, so it's exactly as you left it next time you're back.

Under the Hood

Built Like a Real Product

The small details that separate an arcade from a folder of game files.

⚡

Instant load

No bundlers, no heavy frameworks. Every game opens straight in the browser, on desktop or mobile.

🪪

One shared profile

A single name, level, and XP bar that follows you across all four games — not four separate save files.

💾

Local-first saving

Progress is stored right in your browser. No account, no signup, nothing to lose if a server goes down.

🎚️

Tuned difficulty

Each game is balanced individually rather than copy-pasted, so the challenge actually fits the mechanic.

🔊

Ambient soundtrack

A looping arcade score plays softly in the background, designed to stay out of the way during gameplay.

📱

Responsive by default

The arcade — and every game in it — is built to resize cleanly from a desktop monitor down to a phone screen.
