The Story

One developer.
Four worlds.
Zero shortcuts.

Most online arcades are templates wearing different skins. Yuvraj Soni Arcade is different — every game here is written, tuned, and shipped by a single person, end to end, with no asset packs and no recycled engines.

The goal isn't to have the most games. It's to have four that actually feel considered — distinct mechanics, distinct moods, and a shared profile system that ties your progress across all of them into one evolving identity.

The Loop

How the arcade works

One profile, one XP bar — every game feeds into it.

01

Pick a game

Choose from four titles, each with its own pace and skill curve. No installs, no loading screens — open and play instantly in any browser.

02

Earn XP & coins

Every run you finish feeds your single arcade-wide profile. Performance in any game contributes to the same progress bar.

03

Level up forever

Your level climbs automatically as XP builds. Saved locally — exactly as you left it, every time you return.

Under the Hood

Built like a real product

The small details that separate an arcade from a folder of game files.

⚡

Instant Load

No bundlers, no heavy frameworks. Every game opens straight in the browser, on desktop or mobile, in under a second.

🪪

Shared Profile

One name, one level, one XP bar — across all four games, not four separate save files.

💾

Local-First Save

Progress is stored right in your browser. No account required, nothing lost if a server goes down.

🎮

Real 3D Engines

Two of the four games run on full Three.js 3D engines — not canvas sprites pretending to be 3D.

🔊

Ambient Soundtrack

A looping arcade score plays softly in the background, designed to stay out of the way during gameplay.

📱

Responsive by Default

The arcade and every game in it resize cleanly from a desktop monitor down to a phone screen.
