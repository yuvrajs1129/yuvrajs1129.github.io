The Story

One developer.
Four worlds.
Zero shortcuts.

Most online arcades are templates wearing different skins. Yuvraj Soni Arcade is the opposite — every game here is written, tuned, and shipped by a single person, end to end, with no asset packs and no recycled engines.

The goal isn't to have the most games. It's to have four that feel considered — distinct mechanics, distinct moods, and a shared profile system that ties your progress into one evolving identity.
